Local discovery with restroom information

Know your options before you need a restroom.

Kleenest helps people discover nearby places, understand available restroom information, and make more informed stops. The web app is interactive, and these guides explain how to interpret place results and contribute better information.

From search to a useful stop

1. Discover what's around you

Open Explore to look for locations near your current area or a place you want to visit. Use the map and list together: a nearby business may be worth inspecting even when no restroom information has been contributed yet.

2. Examine the details

Open a location for available restroom information, community observations, reported amenities, and location context. A listing is a starting point for deciding whether a stop suits your needs, not a guarantee of public access.

3. Choose and navigate

Compare practical factors such as distance, accessibility information, family facilities, and reported restrictions. When a place seems suitable, use directions or route planning to reach it.

What place results can — and cannot — tell you

Being on the map and having a confirmed public restroom are different facts. A restaurant, service station, store, or other place can be useful to discover even if nobody has submitted restroom evidence. Kleenest aims to make those distinctions visible rather than hiding every unknown location.

Listed place

The business or point of interest is part of local discovery. A place listing by itself does not establish restroom availability, opening hours, free access, or specific facilities.

Reported information

Community reports can describe practical features such as accessible stalls, changing tables, family restrooms, or purchase requirements. Reports may become outdated and should be read with their context.

Evidence and recency

When available, verification and freshness indicators help you distinguish more recent supporting observations from older or unknown information. Conditions can change even after a visit.

Before a time-sensitive trip: Review the available evidence, check the business's current access rules if needed, and have another option in mind. A map result is not a promise that a restroom is open or usable.

For visitors and local businesses

Kleenest is designed for people looking for practical stop information and for businesses that want accurate location details. Discovery, restroom reporting, and community evidence are separate activities: you can search for places before joining, while contributions may require an account and appropriate permissions.
